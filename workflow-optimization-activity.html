Where This Workflow Gets Stuck
Click any item below to highlight it on the workflow diagram.
⏳
Manager Approval Wait
Up to 48 hrs
Every new hire request sits in a manager's email inbox until they approve it. No reminders, no deadline. It just waits — often over a weekend.
📝
Duplicate Data Entry
30% error rate
HR enters the new hire's info into the HR system. Then IT manually re-types the same info into the account creation system. Same data, entered twice, by two different people.
📬
Manual Email Notifications
Frequently missed
At the end, IT manually emails the new hire, their manager, and their team. This gets forgotten when IT is busy — leaving people unaware that setup is complete.
🔁
No Status Visibility
Causes repeat questions
No one can see where the request is in the process. The new hire's manager keeps emailing IT to ask for updates — each of which takes time to answer manually.

AI Recommendations
The AI analyzed 847 similar onboarding workflows. Here's what it recommends. Accept or reject each one — your choices update the workflow on the left.
Suggestion 1 — Approval Automation
High confidence
Remove the manual manager approval step entirely. Based on patterns from similar workflows, 94% of requests are approved without changes. Auto-approve all requests and notify the manager afterward.
Suggestion 2 — Auto-Sync HR Data
High confidence
Automatically pull new hire data from the HR system when a request is submitted. Eliminates manual re-entry entirely and removes the 30% error rate caused by duplicate typing.
Suggestion 3 — Auto-Notify on Completion
High confidence
Replace the manual email step with automatic notifications sent to the new hire, manager, and team the moment setup is complete. Zero effort, zero chance of being forgotten.
Suggestion 4 — Live Status Tracker
Medium confidence
Add a self-serve status page so managers and new hires can see exactly where their request is. Eliminates the manual "where is my request?" emails to the IT team.
