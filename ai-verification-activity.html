What This Activity Showed

✓

A confident AI output isn't a verified finding. "Confidence: High" means the system is sure about its pattern match — not that the pattern matches your situation.

✓

Verification means checking the recommendation against maintenance records, sensor data, operator observations, and operational history — not just accepting what sounds reasonable.

✓

AI can be partially right and still mislead. The skill isn't rejecting AI — it's knowing which parts of its output hold up and which parts need to be redirected based on what you actually know.
