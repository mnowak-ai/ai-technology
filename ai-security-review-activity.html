Hands-On Activity

Is This AI Prompt Safe?

In real companies, AI tools get used dozens of times a day — and sometimes people accidentally include sensitive information in their prompts. Your job is to spot the risks before they become problems.

Here's what you'll do in this activity:
1
Read a real-looking AI prompt
Each case shows you a prompt that someone at a company sent to an AI tool. It looks normal — but some have hidden problems.
2
Click the risky parts to flag them
Pick a risk type from the toolbar, then click the part of the prompt that contains that risk. It highlights in color.
3
Choose how to fix it
For each problem you find, pick the best way to rewrite it so the AI can still do the job — without the security risk.
The 4 types of risk you're looking for:
Personal Info (PII)
Names, SSNs, card numbers, emails — any data that identifies a real person
Scope Creep
Asking the AI to do or access more than the task actually needs
Insecure Access
Passwords, API keys, or login credentials included in the prompt text
Over-sharing
Internal business data or confidential info that doesn't need to be in the prompt
💡 Heads up: One of the three prompts is actually clean — no problems at all. Part of the skill is knowing when a prompt is safe, not just finding risks.
