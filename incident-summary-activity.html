What This Activity Showed

✓

Raw incident data is always messy. The real skill isn't reading logs — it's deciding which ones matter and which ones are just background noise.

✓

A good summary isn't just shorter — it's structured. What happened, how they got in, what they did, and what's been done is the framework that lets another team act in 30 seconds without reading everything from scratch.

✓

AI tools can help pull together and organize incident data faster than a person reading logs manually — but a human still has to decide what's relevant, what the data means, and what the next team actually needs to know.
